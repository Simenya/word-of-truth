Who we are

A ministry built on the authority of Scripture

Word of Truth Ministries International is a non-profit, faith-based ministry established for the advancement of the Gospel of Jesus Christ. We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired Word of God and the supreme foundation for our faith, doctrine, conduct, ministry and mission.

Rooted in communities, reaching the nations.

Acts 1:8

Vision & mission

What we are working towards

Our vision

To raise a Christ-centred generation grounded in the Word of God, empowered by the Holy Spirit, committed to holy living, equipped for ministry and transformed to transform society.

Our mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach the uncompromised Word of God, make disciples, equip believers for ministry, develop godly leaders, win souls, strengthen families, support vulnerable people and communities, and advance the Kingdom of God locally, nationally and internationally.

“Be diligent to present yourself approved to God, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.”
2 TIMOTHY 2:15
Our objectives

How we pursue our calling

Nine areas of ministry that carry the Gospel from the pulpit into homes, communities and nations.

01

Evangelism

Preaching the Gospel and calling people to repentance, faith and salvation in Jesus Christ.

02

Discipleship

Making disciples who understand Scripture, live by God's Word and reproduce faithful disciples.

03

Bible Teaching

Sound preaching, Bible studies, seminars, conferences and training.

04

Prayer

Promoting individual and corporate prayer, intercession and fasting.

05

Leadership

Identifying, mentoring and equipping faithful men and women for ministry.

06

Missions

Supporting missionary work, church planting and Gospel outreach locally and abroad.

07

Youth & Children

Equipping young people and teaching children the Word in safe, Christ-centred settings.

08

Family

Strengthening marriages and families through teaching, counselling and fellowship.

09

Community Outreach

Demonstrating Christian love through humanitarian aid and support for vulnerable people.
